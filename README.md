# Dungeon Crawler Game

A browser-based dungeon crawler game with combat, enemy AI, and progression system.

## Features

- **Player Movement**: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to move
- **Combat System**: Click or press Spacebar to attack enemies
- **Level Progression**: Gain experience and level up to increase stats
- **Enemy Waves**: Face increasingly difficult enemies as you progress through rooms
- **Inventory System**: Collect gold and potions from defeated enemies
- **Dynamic Difficulty**: Game scales with your progress
- **Particle Effects**: Visual feedback for actions and damage

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Click "START GAME" to begin
3. Move around the dungeon and defeat enemies
4. Collect potions and gold to survive
5. Progress through rooms by defeating all enemies
6. Use the door to advance to the next room

## Controls

- **Movement**: Arrow Keys or WASD
- **Attack**: Spacebar or Click
- **Use Potion**: P
- **Mobile**: D-Pad for movement, Attack and Potion buttons

## Game Stats

- **HP**: Your health - reach 0 to lose
- **Experience**: Gain EXP from defeating enemies to level up
- **Level**: Increases your max HP and damage
- **Gold**: Currency dropped by enemies
- **Potions**: Restore health when used

## Progression

- Each room has more enemies than the last
- Enemies get stronger as rooms progress
- Leveling up increases your maximum HP and damage
- The game continues until you defeat all enemies

## Technical Details

- Built with vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- Real-time collision detection
- Particle system for visual effects
- Enemy AI with pathfinding and combat

Enjoy your dungeon adventure!
